## 6. Step-by-step Launch in Meta Ads Manager
1. Go to **business.facebook.com → Ads Manager → + Create**.
2. Choose **Leads**, name the campaign `Property Wanted – 20k sqft – Malad/Kandivli/Borivali – Nov26`.
3. **Special Ad Category:** select **Housing** (required for real-estate ads in many regions). Note that this limits age, gender and pin-code targeting, so use the location radius instead if Meta enforces it.
4. Ad set: Conversion location **Instant forms**, choose the Facebook Page, then apply the targeting, budget and schedule above.
5. Ad: upload the Canva creative, paste the copy for Variants A/B/C, set the CTA, and attach the Instant Form.
6. Connect the leads to **Meta Business Suite → Leads Center**, or to a CRM/Google Sheet via Zapier/n8n.
7. Publish, then check delivery after 24 hours.
