Privacy Policy

Sannishtha Elder Care, a programme of Sannishtha Foundation · Last updated 13 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what we need to call you back and help plan care.
  • We never sell your data, and we do not show advertising or use tracking cookies.
  • The cost estimator runs in your browser. Your answers reach us only if you choose to send them.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent.

1. Who we are

Sannishtha Elder Care is a programme of Sannishtha Foundation, a not-for-profit company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U85300MH2022NPL394143), with its registered office at A-306, Vishnu Shivam Tower, Thakur Village, Kandivli East, Mumbai 400101, Maharashtra. For this website, Sannishtha Foundation is the “data fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Sannishtha Foundation.

3. Information about your parent or another person

Many families contact us about a parent or relative. If you share details about someone else, please do so with their knowledge, and with their agreement where they are able to give it. Before we provide care management, we speak with the elder directly wherever possible. If an elder cannot make decisions, we work with their lawful guardian or the person they have authorised.

4. What stays on your device

  • Cost estimator. It calculates in your browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you send the contact form with the estimator box ticked.
  • Readiness checklist. Your ticks are saved in your browser’s local storage so they are there when you return. They never reach us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

5. Services that handle your data for us

We use a few outside services to run this website. They process data only to provide their service.

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the contact form to our email inbox.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail), where enquiries are stored, and the fonts on this website. Loading the fonts shares your IP address and browser type with Google.
  • Our website host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages visited, for security.
  • Our bank and payment providers process donations.

Some of these services may store data outside India. We share data with government bodies only where the law requires it, for example for tax certificates, or when you ask us to apply for a scheme on your behalf.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to care management: up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Care management records: for the duration of care and up to 3 years after it ends, unless the law requires longer.
  • Donation records: as long as tax and company law require.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, which stops further use but does not affect what was done before;
  • nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email sannishthafoundation@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We will reply within 30 days.

9. How we protect your data

Access to enquiries and care records is limited to the staff who need it. We use accounts with two-step verification, share files only with named people, and never send health records over public groups. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you before it applies.
