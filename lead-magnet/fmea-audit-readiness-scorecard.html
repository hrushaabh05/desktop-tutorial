    Quick reference: what changed with AIAG‑VDA

    Old way (AIAG 4th ed.)

    Risk Priority Number = S × O × D. A score of 120 could hide a severity‑10 failure. Form‑based worksheets with no structure analysis.

    AIAG‑VDA (2019)

    Action Priority (High / Medium / Low) puts severity first. 7 steps: Planning, Structure, Function, Failure, Risk, Optimisation, Documentation. Failure chains link effect, mode and cause.

    What auditors look for

    The same process steps in the process flow, PFMEA and control plan; special characteristics flagged across all three; actions closed with re‑rated AP; evidence of reviews after changes and complaints.

    Why EV programmes raise the bar

    New HV, battery and thermal failure modes have no FMEA history. OEMs expect foundation FMEAs and lessons learned before PPAP.
