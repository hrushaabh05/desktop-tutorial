## 5. Budget and bidding (starter plan)

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Test budget | INR 1.5–2.5 lakh for 4 weeks across all variants |
| Daily budget per campaign | INR 1,500–2,500 |
| Bidding | Start with Maximum Delivery (automated). Move to Cost Cap once you have ~30 conversions |
| Schedule | Weekdays, 8 am to 8 pm IST |
| Frequency | Refresh creative or copy when frequency exceeds about 4, or CTR falls 30% from its peak |

**Benchmarks to judge results** (typical B2B ranges; your results will vary):
- CTR: 0.5–0.9%
- Landing-page completion: 25–40% of visitors finish the scorecard
- Form submission: 20–35% of completers

**Optimisation rule:** after 2 weeks, pause any variant with CTR below 0.4% or cost per lead above 2× the campaign average, and move its budget to the best-performing variant.

## 6. Tracking setup checklist

- [ ] Install the **LinkedIn Insight Tag** on `syselectechnologies.in`.
- [ ] Create two conversion events: **Scorecard completed**, fired when the results page appears, and **Lead submitted**, fired when the form is sent.
- [ ] Connect the scorecard form (`FORM_ENDPOINT` in the page's `CONFIG`) to your CRM, so leads aren't lost. The form currently falls back to email.
- [ ] Check that the UTM parameters reach your CRM, so you can see which persona variant produced each lead.
- [ ] Optional: run the same offer as a **LinkedIn Lead Gen Form** campaign. Forms are pre-filled from the member's profile, which usually lifts submission rates. Collect name, work email, company, job title, company size, and "Which best describes your BESS plan?" (the five personas). Then send the scorecard link in the thank-you message.

## 7. Compliance notes

- All statistics in the ads come from the IESA *India ESS Market Update, September 2026* (308 GWh tendered, 17× growth, 13.7 GWh installed, 122 GWh in execution, ~19% of capacity cancelled, tariff range, 23% EPC share, the proposed CEA grid-forming rule). Keep a copy on file in case LinkedIn asks for substantiation.
- "1 in 5 tenders cancelled" rounds IESA's figure of ~19% of tendered capacity (41 of 215 tenders, also 19%).
- Make sure the landing page links to a privacy policy before the campaign goes live. LinkedIn requires one for lead collection.
